2. Description of Service

Gemini Watermark Remover is a free, client-side web application that uses inverse alpha compositing to mathematically reverse visible AI-generated watermarks from images and video files. All processing occurs entirely within your web browser; no media data is transmitted to external servers.

The tool is designed to process watermarks from Google Gemini (Imagen 3 / Nano Banana) images and Google Veo 3, Flow, and Gemini Omni video outputs.
