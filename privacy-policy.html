1. Client-Side Media Processing (Zero Server Uploads)

Unlike cloud-based editors or AI inpainting services, Gemini Watermark Remover runs entirely within your web browser. When you import an image (PNG, JPG, WebP) or video (MP4, WebM, MOV):

  • No Server Transmission: Files are loaded directly into your browser's local memory via the HTML5 File and Blob APIs. They are never uploaded to our servers, third-party cloud providers, or any external storage systems.
  • Local GPU Processing: Frame decoding, inverse alpha compositing calculations, and video re-encoding are executed directly on your device using HTML5 Canvas, OffscreenCanvas, and WebCodecs hardware acceleration.
  • Automatic Cleanup: When you close the tab, refresh the page, or navigate away, all processed frames and temporary memory buffers are instantly released by your browser's garbage collector.
Zero Knowledge Architecture: We have neither the technical capability nor the intent to view, store, monitor, or analyze your media files. Your photographs, creative assets, and confidential materials remain strictly on your device at all times.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require registration, login, or any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to use this tool. The only data involved is:

  • Standard Server Logs: GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) automatically records standard web server access logs including IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and timestamps for infrastructure reliability.
  • Local Session Preferences: We use sessionStorage to remember your active panel tab selection (Image vs. Video) during your browser session. This data never leaves your device.
  • Voluntary Communications: If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we receive the information you deliberately submit to assist you.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Gemini Watermark Remover does not use proprietary tracking cookies for its core watermark removal functionality. Third-party services integrated on the site (such as font CDNs or analytics) may place cookies on your browser.

You can disable or selectively manage cookies in your browser settings or Internet security software. Disabling cookies does not affect the local watermark erasure tool's functionality.

5. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

For users residing in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679) include:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of your data processing.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pi.seotools@gmail.com.

6. CCPA / CPRA Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is being collected and how it is used.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information. Since all media processing occurs locally in your browser, we possess no user-generated content to share or sell.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Gemini Watermark Remover does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us immediately for deletion.

8. Data Retention & Security

Since no user-generated media data is ever transmitted to or stored on our servers, there is effectively zero data to retain or secure on our end. Your files exist solely in your browser's local memory during your active session and are automatically discarded when you navigate away.

For voluntary communications (emails, GitHub issues), we retain correspondence only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry.
