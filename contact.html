Quick Support FAQ

• Why didn't a video download complete?

Video processing uses hardware WebCodecs. On very large 4K files, older mobile devices may exhaust available memory. Try closing background tabs or processing on a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, or Brave recommended).

• How do I report a DMCA copyright concern?

Please send complete details including the URL, identification of the copyrighted work, and authorization proof to pi.seotools@gmail.com. See our Disclaimer & DMCA Policy for full instructions.

• Can I request support for a new AI model watermark?

Absolutely! If Google releases new Gemini or Veo model variants with different watermark positions or opacities, open a GitHub issue or message us with sample dimensions so we can calibrate a new preset.
